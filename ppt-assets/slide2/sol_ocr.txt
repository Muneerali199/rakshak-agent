PROBLEM SOLUTION

e Canonical Fusion — one CCTNS-shaped schema unifies
FIRs, CDRs, financial trails; no legacy migration.

e Identity Resolution — 4-feature hybrid scoring resolves names
across Hindi, English and Hinglish.

e District Vault Mesh — signed queries travel between vaults;
FIR data never leaves its district.

e Warrant Gate — protected victim data unmasks only behind
a dual-signed, scoped, expiring warrant.

e Tamper-Evident Ledgers — reviews, warrants and mesh exchanges
are SHA-256 hash-chained.

e RakshakAI Self-Security — a 14B fine-tuned model scans the
platform's own code for vulnerabilities.
